Immersia 360 is a modern Digital Twin application providing an immersive, interactive, and realistic virtual representation of the Techno Institute of Engineering & Management campus.

Unlike traditional galleries, Immersia 360 places users inside a 360-degree virtual environment, enabling free exploration and navigation.

Demonstrating the practical application of computer graphics, WebGL, VR concepts, and interactive software design.
